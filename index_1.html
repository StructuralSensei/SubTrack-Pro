User Guide

Learn the interface in about two minutes.

Getting around

Menu bar

  • Dashboard shows totals, projections, renewals and recommendations.
  • Subscriptions lists, searches, sorts and edits every entry.
  • Reminders shows renewals that fall inside their reminder window.
  • Settings sets your role, currency, and data tools.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • N add a new subscription
  • / jump to search
  • Esc close any open dialog
  • Ctrl/⌘ + Z undo recent changes (up to 50), except while typing in a field

Working with data

Adding a subscription

  • Required fields are name, cost, cycle, and next renewal date.
  • The form warns you about invalid values and likely duplicates.
  • The monthly equivalent updates as you type.

Reminders

  • Set a lead time (1–30 days) per subscription.
  • Enable browser notifications in Settings to get alerts when the app is open.

Privacy

  • All data stays in your browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Export a JSON backup regularly, because clearing site data deletes everything.

Interface elements

  • Gray shimmer blocks mean content is loading. They disappear once data is ready.
  • Coloured pills show status: green is more than a week away, amber is four to seven days away, red is three days or fewer, and "unused" is shown in red beside any subscription you marked as not used.
  • Bars show each category's share of your total monthly spend.
  • Blue highlight ring during the tour points to the element being explained. Press Next or Skip to continue.

About

Version 1.0.0

SubTrack Pro helps freelancers, business owners and engineers track the software and AI tools they pay for, so they can see what they spend and cut what they don't use.

It is a single HTML file. It needs no account, no server and no internet connection after the page loads. Your data never leaves your device.
